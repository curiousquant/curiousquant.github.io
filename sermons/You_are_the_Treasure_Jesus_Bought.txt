You are the Treasure Jesus Bought
Matthew 13:44-46
The Parable of the Hidden Treasure
	44 “The kingdom of heaven is like treasure hidden in a field, which a man found and covered up. Then in his joy he goes and sells all that he has and buys that field.
The Parable of the Pearl of Great Value
	45 “Again, the kingdom of heaven is like a merchant in search of fine pearls, 46 who, on finding one pearl of great value, went and sold all that he had and bought it.

Here there are two parables about hidden treasure and a pearl of great value. Nowadays, if you knew about a stock that would go up many times its value, would you invest all you have into it? Here we see treasure that was hidden, and a man bought it with all he had. You are the treasure that God bought with His own Son’s life. You are the hidden treasure in a field, and the pearl of great price. God bought you with His own Son’s blood, that you may know Him and be saved. 
	How many times have we missed something important in our lives? Losing a key, missing important information for a test, or even missing out in church? Can you believe that God gave His only Son for you, and saw you like a precious pearl of great price and chose you as a people for His own possession? (Titus 2:14) If you saw someone you like, would you try your best to date her/him? Jesus came running after us, coming down to earth in His advent, entering His ministry and giving up His own life for us on the cross, that we could be redeemed and be with Him in heaven forever. There is no greater love that if someone dies for his friends. (John 15:13). “Jesus paid it all, all to Him we owe, sin had left a crimson stain, He washed it white as snow.” He loved us as we are, in our wretched sinful state, He saw you and made you, and wrote all your days before you were born. (Psalm 139). Jesus calls you to believe in Him and wants you to believe in Him because He loves you. He showed this through the cross. It isn’t fiction but real, Jesus really went to the cross, suffering innocently for the sake of the world. He chose to take the nails in His hands and the flogging and the mocking, all for your sake that by His wounds we are healed (Isaiah 53:5). If you want to see what it was like, try watching The Passion of Christ or The Chosen. Can you imagine what He did for you? And not only that He gave the Holy Spirit as our guarantee, that through faith we may be sanctified becoming like Him (Ephesians 1:13-14). God himself is with us, watching over us and straightening our crooked paths and ways, helping us be like Christ. When you believe and are baptized, you appeal to God for a new conscious and become a new creation (1 Peter 3:21). 
17 Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come:[a] The old has gone, the new is here! (2 Corinthians 5:17). 
Jesus lives in you and your old self is gone. All your sins forgiven by the blood of the Lamb. Jesus saved you from hell, the eternal punishment of God’s wrath, where His Son went for us taking on the Father’s own wrath for our sake, to have mercy on us and be our righteousness (Jeremiah 23:6). It is by grace through faith you have been saved, not by works lest one should boast (Ephesians 2:8-9). By God’s mercy we are saved and are free from the shackles of sin that bind you, that we have the power to not sin, not of ourselves, but from God who is with you helping you overcome.

7 But we have this treasure in jars of clay to show that this all-surpassing power is from God and not from us. (2 Corinthians 4:7). 

Let us put our trust in our Maker, always having hope in whatever may come that He will make a way, when there seems to be no way. Let us overcome the trials of this life with faith, always trusting in Jesus to be there for us in our troubles. Jesus said in John 16:33:

I have said these things to you, that in me you may have peace. In the world you will have tribulation. But take heart; I have overcome the world.”

For the moment all discipline seems painful rather than pleasant, but later it yields the peaceful fruit of righteousness to those who have been trained by it. (Hebrews 12:11)

2 Count it all joy, my brothers,[a] when you meet trials of various kinds, 3 for you know that the testing of your faith produces steadfastness. 4 And let steadfastness have its full effect, that you may be perfect and complete, lacking in nothing. (James 1:2-4).

 God is working in your life to sanctify you and help your faith to increase. If you need more faith, then ask God to help with your unbelief just as it is written in Mark 9:23-25: 
23 And Jesus said to him, “‘If you can’! All things are possible for one who believes.” 24 Immediately the father of the child cried out[a] and said, “I believe; help my unbelief!” 25 And when Jesus saw that a crowd came running together, he rebuked the unclean spirit, saying to it, “You mute and deaf spirit, I command you, come out of him and never enter him again.”
Jesus loves you and forever bought you by His blood, securing you as His own where nothing can separate us from the love of Christ. Romans 8:35-39:
35 Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? Shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or danger, or sword? 36 As it is written,
“For your sake we are being killed all the day long;
    we are regarded as sheep to be slaughtered.”
37 No, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him who loved us. 38 For I am sure that neither death nor life, nor angels nor rulers, nor things present nor things to come, nor powers, 39 nor height nor depth, nor anything else in all creation, will be able to separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord.

Let us always plead for mercy to God and never become legalistic thinking that we are better than others by our works, but trust in His mercy, in that we are sinners in need of a Savior (Luke 18:9-14). Jesus is with us-let us trust Him, bearing our crosses in our daily lives. Jesus loves you!!!
