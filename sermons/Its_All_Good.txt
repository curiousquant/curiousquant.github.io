It’s All Good
Romans 8:28-30
28And we know that for those who love God all things work together for good, for those who are called according to his purpose. 29For those whom he foreknew he also predestined to be conformed to the image of his Son, in order that he might be the firstborn among many brothers. 30And those whom he predestined he also called, and those whom he called he also justified, and those whom he justified he also glorified. 

God works all things for our good, for those who love Him and are called according to His purpose. In every trial we went through God has taken care of us bringing us closer to Him and relying on His power. No one is without trials in this world, but as Jesus said in John 16:33 

33I have said these things to you, that in me you may have peace. In the world you will have tribulation. But take heart; I have overcome the world.”

We can take heart knowing that Jesus has overcome the world and all authority and power is under Him. In 1 Corinthians 15:27 apostle Paul writes,

27For “God has put all things in subjection under his feet.” But when it says, “all things are put in subjection,” it is plain that he is excepted who put all things in subjection under him.

God is sovereign over all, omniscient, omnipresent, omnipotent, and holds all things together by His word. Colossians 1:15-17 says,
15He is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation. 16For by him all things were created, in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible, whether thrones or dominions or rulers or authorities-all things were created through him and for him. 17And he is before all things, and in him all things hold together. 18And he is the head of the body, the church. He is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead, that in everything he might be preeminent. 19For in him all the fullness of God was pleased to dwell, 20and through him to reconcile to himself all things, whether on earth or in heaven, making peace by the blood of his cross.
We can hold onto Christ because He holds it all in His hands. We have a great God in heaven!!! Being a Christian doesn’t mean that we will not be without trials. Jesus said that we would have tribulation, but said, “take heart; I have overcome the world.” So we can trust in Jesus who holds us in His hands. Jesus loves you more than you can ever imagine. “If grace is an ocean, we are all sinking.” (David Crowder Band-How He Loves). He loves you so much that He died for you-He took on a crown of thorns on his brow, Roman nails in His hands and feet, suffered 39 lashes by whips for you and went through the torment of hell, all to save you from hell. God’s Son came down to earth, taking the form a servant to save you. Philippians 2:7-11 says,

7but emptied himself, by taking the form of a servant, being born in the likeness of men. 8And being found in human form, he humbled himself by becoming obedient to the point of death, even death on a cross. 9Therefore God has highly exalted him and bestowed on him the name that is above every name, 10so that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth, 11and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

	Romans 8:30 says

 30And those whom he predestined he also called, and those whom he called he also justified, and those whom he justified he also glorified.

For those of us who believe in Jesus, we are justified by Jesus’s atoning sacrifice on the cross. He took your punishment saving you from hell by taking your place as a final sacrifice for sins. Hebrews 7:27 says,

27He has no need, like those high priests, to offer sacrifices daily, first for his own sins and then for those of the people, since he did this once for all when he offered up himself.
 
Apostle Paul also writes “those whom he justified he also glorified.” Having been justified, we are glorified becoming like the image of Christ with the Holy Spirit as our guarantee. 2 Corinthians 1:21-22 says,

21And it is God who establishes us with you in Christ, and has anointed us, 22and who has also put his seal on us and given us his Spirit in our hearts as a guarantee. 

Isaiah 35:10 says,

10And the ransomed of the LORD shall return
	and come to Zion with singing;
everlasting joy shall be upon their heads;
	they shall obtain gladness and joy;
	and sorrow and sighing shall flee away.

We have a great hope in Jesus Christ who won the victory over sin and death! All things work together for our good for those who love God and who are called according to His purpose. God has overcome the world, so we can take heart and trust in God to move mountains for us through faith (Matthew 17:20). We can overcome with faith! It is our shield to protect us (Ephesians 6:16). Proverbs 3:5-8 says,

5Trust in the LORD with all your heart,
	and do not learn on your own understanding.
6In all your ways acknowledge him,
	and he will make straight your paths.
7Be not wise in your own eyes;
	fear the LORD, and turn away from evil.
8It will be healing to your flesh
	and refreshment to your bones.

Those who are called are justified and glorified. God loves you!
